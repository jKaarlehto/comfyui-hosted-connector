Hosted ComfyUI

Connect to the workspace you were invited to. Its WebUI will open when ready.

Download and install Open ComfyUI

Install the connector to continue. Allow local access if your browser asks.

Local address for your browser and Notch

  1. Open HostedComfyUIConnector.exe from your browser's downloads menu, usually near the upper right.
  2. Confirm installation for your Windows account.
  3. This page will switch to Connect automatically.

The connector starts the server and keeps a private local connection open for your browser and Notch. Startup can take a few minutes.

Keep the connector open while working. Keep your invitation link private.
