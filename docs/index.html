Installation help

Install the Windows connector

  1. Download or reinstall the connector.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Look in your browser's downloads menu, usually near the upper right, or your Downloads folder.
  3. Finish installation. This tab will try to continue automatically; click Connect if your browser asks.

Windows may ask you to confirm opening a downloaded application.

App Installer download is also available. Open the downloaded file to install.

The connector starts the server and keeps a private local connection open for your browser and Notch. Startup can take a few minutes.

Keep the connector open while working. Keep your invitation link private.
